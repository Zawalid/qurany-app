# Third-party notices

Qurany bundles, streams or learns from the work of others. This file lists each item, the terms it was found under,
and what is still unconfirmed. It reflects the research done for the project as of 2026-11-01. It is a record, not
legal advice.

Status words used below:

- **Cleared**: the terms found allow this use, and the credit is given here and in the app.
- **Not yet confirmed**: no licence was found, or the terms found do not cover redistribution. Permission is being
  requested. Until a written yes arrives, the item is used for personal use only and is not cleared for a public build.

## Network services the app calls

These are not bundled content. They are contacted at run time.

- everyayah.com: recitation audio, when you play or download a recitation.
- api.aladhan.com: prayer times, only if you turn on exact times. Your approximate location is sent.
- Apple speech recognition: your voice, only while you recite aloud and only if you allow online recognition.
